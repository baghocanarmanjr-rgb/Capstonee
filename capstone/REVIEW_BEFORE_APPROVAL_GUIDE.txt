REVIEW-BEFORE-APPROVAL VIEWING MODE
===================================

Purpose
-------
Every approval now starts from a read-only review page. Users no longer approve
APP, PR, AOQ, PO, NLP, IAR, RIS, or ICS directly from the compact record table.

Covered processes
-----------------
1. APP — review APP items and the source PPMP items.
2. PR — review the final PR requirements and source PPMP items.
3. AOQ — review the approved PR beside the OCR-reviewed winning offer, supplier
   records, and raw OCR text.
4. PO — review the linked PR, approved AOQ offer, and generated PO items.
5. PR–PO NLP Verification — review PR items, PO items, the NLP result, confidence,
   finding, recommended action, and full comparison details.
6. IAR — review PO items and all delivery/acceptance items before adding anything
   to Inventory.
7. RIS/ICS — review every item to be issued or assigned.

Required approval behavior
--------------------------
- The list page button opens "Review All Items & Approve".
- The review page is read-only.
- Every item displays its line identifier, source item identifier, description,
  specification, quantity, unit, unit cost, amount, and every stored source field.
- Linked procurement identifiers are shown at the top.
- The final Approve button remains disabled until the reviewer checks the
  confirmation box.
- Approval endpoints accept POST only and reject attempts that do not contain the
  review confirmation value.
- Approved records remain viewable but cannot be approved again.

The review mode preserves the existing offline workflow and SQLite database.
