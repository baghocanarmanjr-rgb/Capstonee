COA WORKBOOK INTEGRATION WITH AI-ONLY PR–PO NLP VERIFICATION

1. The inventory workbook remains the source of items. It does not display an anomaly score.
2. Every valid worksheet and row in data/COA_Inventory_Dataset.xlsx is read dynamically.
3. Real article, description, unit, and specification text form the domain corpus.
4. Controlled four-class examples are generated: Acceptable, Needs Review, Needs Recanvass, Reject.
5. Controlled issue labels are also generated for specification, quantity, unit, missing-description,
   different-item, minor wording, and incomplete-description discrepancies.
6. Generated examples are saved to data/COA_Generated_PR_PO_Training_Pairs.csv for audit/review.
7. Existing manually labelled PR–PO examples are merged with the COA-derived examples.
8. FastText learns Asuncion item terminology. Trained Logistic Regression classifiers directly
   predict the final status and primary issue.
9. No hardcoded rule, hybrid score, threshold mapping, live reference override, or rules fallback
   controls the final result.
10. The only user-facing discrepancy decision remains in PR–PO NLP Verification.
11. COA-derived pairs are synthetic augmentation. Add real reviewed PR–PO outcomes and use an
    untouched real holdout set for final external accuracy reporting.
